# Denboku Shōgi

> **Denboku Shōgi** (電墨将棋) is a shōgi (Japanese chess) game for jailbroken Kindles, made for e-ink. Play the Fairy-Stockfish engine, a friend on the same Kindle or over Wi-Fi, or people on Lishogi.

## Download

  - [Download Denboku Shōgi for Kindle (Latest Release)](https://github.com/Jefik/denboku-shogi/releases/latest/download/denboku-kindle.zip)

Also available for Linux, Windows and Android on the [releases page](https://github.com/Jefik/denboku-shogi/releases/latest).

## Features

  - Nine variants: standard shōgi, Minishōgi, Kyotoshōgi, Checkshōgi, Dōbutsu, Goro Goro, Tori shōgi, Judkins shōgi and Euroshōgi, all played by Fairy-Stockfish at ten levels.
  - Handicap games, clocks (byoyomi, increment, sudden death), hints and undo.
  - A tutorial played on the board, from how the pieces move to castles and tesuji, and 400 tsume (mate) problems.
  - Game analysis with an evaluation graph and the engine's best moves.
  - LAN games with chat, Lishogi and CSA servers such as Floodgate.
  - KIF, CSA and SFEN; the last 200 games kept.
  - Made for e-ink: black and white, redraws only what changed, big touch targets.
  - English, Japanese and Brazilian Portuguese.

## Installation

  1. Download `denboku-kindle.zip` and unzip it.
  2. Copy the `denboku/` folder to `/mnt/us/extensions/` on your Kindle.
  3. Launch via **KUAL -> 電墨将棋 Denboku Shōgi -> Play**.
  4. Optional: **Add shortcut to the library** in the same menu puts the game in the library.

## Good to Know

  - Needs a jailbroken Kindle with KUAL or Jobman, on hard-float firmware (5.16.3 or later).
  - The game can check GitHub for new versions and update itself; it asks the first time.
  - Settings and the game in progress are kept in `/mnt/us/extensions/denboku/`, saved games in `/mnt/us/documents/shogi/`.
