# Kindle Jailbreaking & Modding Guide

> New here? This page explains what jailbreaking a Kindle means, why people do it, how to get started, and what you can do afterward. KindleModShelf.me collects Kindle mods in one place, so you do not have to hunt through scattered forum posts.

## What Is Jailbreaking?

A Kindle is a good e-ink screen that Amazon keeps locked to its own store and software. **Jailbreaking** is a software-only change that lets you run your own code on the device. You do not open the Kindle or change any hardware.

Once it is jailbroken, you can install alternative apps, change how the device looks and behaves, and stop Amazon from deciding things for you. Your Kindle still works as a normal Kindle.

## Why Jailbreak Your Kindle?

![video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qtk7ERwlIAk)

  - **Your books stay yours:** Amazon cannot change, remove, or restrict your files after jailbreaking.
  - **A far better reader:** Install **KOReader** for EPUB, PDF, CBZ and more, with fine control over fonts, margins, layout and page turns.
  - **Cloud features back:** Amazon has started to remove cloud features from older Kindles. Jailbreaking lets you replace them with Google Drive or self-hosted options.
  - **Privacy and fewer ads:** Remove lock screen ads and block tracking.
  - **You decide about updates:** Block forced firmware updates so a new version cannot break your setup.
  - **Make it yours:** Custom screensavers, custom menus, and interface tweaks.
  - **More than reading:** Games, a music player, drawing and note apps, a terminal, a file manager and more.
  - **A longer life for old Kindles:** Mods keep older devices useful after Amazon stops supporting them.

## Before You Start

:::warning
Jailbreaking is safe when you follow the guide, but mistakes can leave a Kindle unusable. Read each step before you do it, and do not skip the update-blocking steps.
:::

  - **Check your model and firmware.** Which jailbreak you can use depends on both. The wiki has a tool that tells you which one fits your device.
  - **Stay offline until you know.** A Kindle that updates itself can lose the ability to be jailbroken, so follow the wiki's advice on preventing automatic updates.
  - **Use the wiki's guide, not a random video.** Methods change as Amazon changes its firmware, and the wiki is kept up to date.

## How To Jailbreak

The [Kindle Modding Wiki](https://kindlemodding.org/jailbreaking/) is the up-to-date guide for jailbreaking every supported Kindle. Start there. It helps you find the right method for your device and walks you through it step by step.

When you finish, you are ready to install apps.

## Installing and Opening Apps

Modern jailbreaks come with **KPM**, a package manager that installs apps for you. The wiki explains how to use it in its [Installing Homebrew guide](https://kindlemodding.org/jailbreaking/whats-next/installing-homebrew.html).

Apps usually appear in one of two ways:

  - **Scriptlets:** These show up as books in your library. Tap them to run.
  - **KUAL:** Open the KUAL booklet and tap the app you want to run.

Only install things from sources you trust. Mods run code on your device.
