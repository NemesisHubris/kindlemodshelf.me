# Deduko
> **Deduko** is a detective logic-grid puzzle game for jailbroken Kindles. Each case gives you suspects, weapons, rooms, motives and arrival times, plus a list of clues: mark the grid until every suspect is matched, by pure logic and without guessing. Built for e-ink, fully offline, in English and Italian.

## Download
  - [Deduko on GitHub](https://github.com/nardoman/deduko)
  - [Latest release](https://github.com/nardoman/deduko/releases/latest)

## Features
  - 5 handmade cases, from one ball to five (3x4 up to 5x5 grids)
  - Endless generated cases, always with exactly one solution
  - Classic staircase grid with X and dot marks, Auto-exclude and Undo
  - Error mode: at most 5 wrong marks, then the case starts over
  - Case codes to replay or share a generated grid
  - English and Italian; new languages and case packs are added as files
  - Fully offline

## Installation
**With KPM (Wi-Fi):**
  1. Download `deduko_kpm_setup.sh` from the [latest release](https://github.com/nardoman/deduko/releases/latest).
  2. Connect your Kindle by USB and copy it into the `documents` folder.
  3. Eject the Kindle, turn Wi-Fi on and open "Deduko - KPM setup" from the library. It adds the Deduko KPM repository and installs the game.
  4. Open "Deduko" from the library. Later versions install with `;kpm upgrade`.

**Without KPM (USB only):**
  1. Download `deduko_<version>_manuale.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/nardoman/deduko/releases/latest).
  2. Extract it into the root of the Kindle storage (the folder that contains `documents`).
  3. Eject the Kindle and open "Deduko" from the library.

## How to Play
  1. Pick a case from the home screen, or a new generated case of 1 to 5 balls.
  2. Choose Free play or Max 5 errors, then tap Start.
  3. Pick X (no) or dot (yes) in the top bar and tap the grid cells. Tap a mark again to erase it.
  4. Read the clues and tap one to cross it out once used.
  5. The case is solved when every suspect has exactly one item of each category.
